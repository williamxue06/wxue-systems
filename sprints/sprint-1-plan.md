# Sprint 1 Plan

**Goal:** A user can log a workout by entering the exercise, weight, and reps.

**Why this:** This helps users track their gym progress instead of relying on memory, so they can see how their weights and reps change over time.

**Done looks like:** A user can save an exercise with its weight and reps, then find it in their workout history after refreshing the page.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3
