# Lab 1 Reflection

## Question 1

What surprised me most was how much Claude could do from a short prompt. It created the app files, installed Streamlit, and ran checks to see whether the app worked. I didn’t realize it could handle that many steps without me writing the code myself. It didn’t do anything I didn’t request.

## Question 2

I added the ability to edit expenses and set a monthly budget. Editing is useful because someone might enter the wrong amount, choose the wrong category, or select the wrong date. It lets them fix that mistake without deleting the expense and entering everything again. The monthly budget helps users keep track of their spending limit. Both features worked, and nothing that worked before broke.

## Question 3

One missing requirement is filtering expenses by month. A real user would probably want to view spending for a specific month instead of looking through every expense. This would make it easier to compare spending between months and understand where their money went.
