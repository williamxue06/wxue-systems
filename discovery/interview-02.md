# Participant 2

Responses supplied by the student and confirmed as coming from a real interview. Wording preserved; identifying names omitted. Interview date and method were not provided.

**How do you track now?**

A Google Sheet on my phone with planned weights and rep ranges for each training block.

**Last time you forgot?**

A couple of weeks ago on Romanian deadlifts when I swapped in a different bar and lost track of my top set from the week before.

**What’s annoying?**

Pinching and zooming around small spreadsheet cells between sets, especially when I change exercise order because a machine is taken.

**What would make it useful?**

Easy exercise swaps that still keep my history, clear visual cues when I hit the top of a rep range to bump the weight up, and fast logging without lots of taps.
